Lost Baggage Inquiry

遗失行李处理与失主联络
Instructions for Staff & Finders / 工作人员与拾得人指引
  • Report current status or location: Please specify where the item is stored or held (e.g., Lost & Found desk, counter, gate). 请填写行李当前存放位置或保管部门（如失物招领处、值机柜台、登机口等）。
  • Direct transmission: Transmitted reports are dispatched immediately to the owner's emergency contact channel. 提交的信息将直接发送至失主的紧急联络通道。
  • Airport / Station procedure: If turned over to custody, providing a reference or tracking number helps the passenger claim it. 如已移交官方失物招领中心，建议附上登记单号或联系电话以便核对认领。
